CrisisAid Route Planner

An algorithm-centered humanitarian response demo for choosing safer supply delivery routes during conflict or post-conflict emergencies.

Scenario

A relief team needs to send supplies from a supply center to a shelter. Roads have different distances and danger levels. The app uses a modified Dijkstra algorithm to recommend the lowest-cost path.
